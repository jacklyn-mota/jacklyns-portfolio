Biography

I'm a Human Systems Engineering student at Arizona State University specializing in User Experience. I'm interested in how design, technology, and human behavior intersect to create digital experiences that are intuitive, accessible, and engaging.

My Approach to Design

I enjoy working with HTML and CSS, particularly the visual design and layout decisions that shape how people experience a website. Through my coursework, I've also developed an understanding of professional web development practices, including organizing code, maintaining consistent commits, and documenting project progress.

When I encounter a challenge, I start by identifying what I know and breaking the problem into manageable steps. I document questions, refer to my notes, and research possible solutions before revisiting anything I don't immediately understand. This process helps me approach unfamiliar tasks with patience, curiosity, and a willingness to learn.

What Inspires Me

My experience working with students with dyslexia has influenced how I think about accessibility and different learning needs. It has encouraged me to consider how people interact with information and how thoughtful design can make digital experiences more intuitive and inclusive.

Looking Ahead

I'm continuing to develop my JavaScript skills and build confidence creating interactive experiences. As I explore my long-term career interests, I hope to combine visual design, usability, and accessibility to create digital products that better support the people who use them.
